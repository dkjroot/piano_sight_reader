The 30 MP3 files are copied unchanged from the Tone.js audio collection,
covering MIDI A0–C8 in minor thirds. SHA-256 hashes and pinned download URLs are
recorded in manifest.json. The original instrument has multiple velocity layers;
this browser rendering contains one sampled layer. Playback here pitch-shifts
between adjacent recordings and changes gain with incoming velocity. It does not
include the original instrument’s release, pedal or resonance sample engine.

These recordings retain their CC BY 3.0 licence as separate media distributed
