# Bundled music

The MIDI files and pre-ingested performance data in this directory retain their
individual music licences, separately from the GPL-3.0 application code. No recording
is bundled. Sources are editions explicitly shared by the Mutopia Project.

Seven editions carry Mutopia contributor public-domain dedications. Rachmaninoff's
Prelude is CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and share-alike terms retained for its MIDI
and inspection JSON; the full licence is in [CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt](CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The catalogue records download URLs, declared edition,
contributor, permission evidence, review date and SHA-256 of each unmodified MIDI.
Additional metadata-page and archive hashes are retained where available. Source
labels are recorded as published; readable library titles identify movements.

JSON files are technical inspections produced by `score_import.py`, with provenance
added. The MIDI music is unchanged; no new arrangement or recovered printed notation
is claimed. Subsequent musical adaptations of the CC BY-SA edition must retain its
attribution and satisfy its share-alike terms. Mutopia and the contributors do not
endorse this application. The music is supplied as-is without warranties; see the
individual licence and the source edition disclaimer.
